I have been building something for my own nights out, and it is ready for other people to try.

Noctography answers two questions: is tonight worth the drive, and if it is, what should you point at. These are real screens from tonight, not mock-ups: cloud, moonlight, when astronomical dark starts and ends, an honest aurora read, the meteor rate at your own sky, and a ranked list of what is worth pointing at.

It runs in a browser on any phone. It is free, there is no account, and it does not want your email.

Swipe through, then have a look at your own sky. Link in bio, and I would genuinely like to hear what is missing.
