Premiere:
- Blend mode Normal. No blend change needed, the alpha does the work.
- Do not use Screen or Add: the type carries a dark #050418 shadow so it
  holds against stars, and Screen would erase it.
- Scale 100%, no auto-fit. Cross-dissolve 8 to 12 frames in and out.
- Text sits inside the Instagram safe area: 200px top, 400px bottom,
  180px right. Nudge horizontally, not vertically, if a card lands on you.
- If the edit runs nearer 15 seconds, drop card 02.
- Export silent, add music at upload.
