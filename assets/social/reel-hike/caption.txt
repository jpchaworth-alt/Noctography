Two and a half kilometres in, most of it uphill, with the tripod on the back and the sky forecast to do exactly one thing.

The thing nobody tells you about night photography is how much of it is walking. And the walk is the bit you cannot undo: by the time you find out the sky has hazed over, you are an hour from the car with a bag on your back.

So the app answers it before I set off. Cloud hour by hour for where I am going, not the town I left. Moonlight, and what it costs the sky. Haze and light pollution counted into a real Bortle number rather than the atlas one. And how close the air is to its dew point, which is the difference between a clean lens at two in the morning and a wasted night.

Free, in your browser, no account. Link in bio, or type noctography.net.
