10 May 2024. The biggest geomagnetic storm in twenty years, and half the country found out about it the next morning.

That is the problem with a night like this. It comes round once or twice a decade, so nobody sensible sits up watching for it. And on almost every other night the honest answer is no, which is exactly why you stop checking.

So I built something that checks for me. Noctography watches the numbers, tells me the night the odds actually change, and shows me where the cloud is not, so I am not driving an hour to stand under an overcast sky. It runs in your browser, there is no account, and it works everything out on your own device.

Free, link in bio, or type noctography.net.
