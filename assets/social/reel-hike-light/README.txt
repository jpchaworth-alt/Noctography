Premiere:
- Blend mode Normal. Not Multiply or Darken: the halo behind the type is
  near-white and a darkening blend would remove it, leaving the letters to
  fight the sky.
- Scale 100%, no auto-fit. Cross-dissolve 8 to 12 frames in and out.
- Text sits inside the Instagram safe area: 200px top, 400px bottom, 180px right.
- Use this set where the frame runs bright: dusk sky, snow, chalk, a pale
  hillside. Switch to the dark set the moment the frame goes dark, and do not
  mix the two within one cut.
- The app tiles stay dark by design, so they read as objects laid on the scene.
- Card 01's mono line (2.4 km, 210 m, 40 min) is placeholder. Send real numbers.
- Caption and alt text are the same as the dark set: see ../reel-hike/.
