Bortle 4 does not mean tonight is Bortle 4.

The maps grade a place on a perfect moonless night. It has nothing to say about the night you are actually driving out into, and that is the night you have to shoot.

So I built the sum into the app. It takes the atlas value for your coordinates, then counts what tonight costs you: moonlight, haze and humidity, and town glow bouncing back off thin high cloud. Each one in magnitudes per square arcsecond, added up, turned back into a Bortle grade and quoted as the median across the dark hours, so one bright half-hour does not define the night.

Tonight my field is a Bortle 4 site reading Bortle 6. Two whole grades, and the only reason is a 95% moon. Across the next nine nights the same field runs from B4 to B8 without moving an inch.
