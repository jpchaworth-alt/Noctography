I have been building something for my own nights out, and it is ready for other people to try.

Noctography answers two questions: is tonight worth the drive, and if it is, what should you point at. Cloud, moonlight, when real darkness starts and ends, where the Milky Way core sits, an honest aurora read, a sixty-night meteor outlook, planetary conjunctions, ISS passes, star trail and NPF exposure maths, and a log for what you actually shot.

It runs in a browser on any phone. It is free, there is no account, and it does not want your email.

Swipe through for what it looks like. Link in bio, and I would genuinely like to hear what is missing.
