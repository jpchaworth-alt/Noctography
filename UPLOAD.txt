Noctography 1.1.2, 31 August 2026

Two files changed since the version that is live (1.1). Upload them to your repository,
keeping the folder structure exactly as it is here:

  app/index.html            ->  app/index.html
  noctography-terrain.js    ->  noctography-terrain.js   (repository root)

On GitHub: Add file -> Upload files, drag both over, commit. Two minutes later the site
is live. Hard refresh once on your phone if it still looks old.

Nothing else needs uploading. The other modules are unchanged; the script tags now read
?v=1.1.2 but that only tells the browser to re-check them, it does not need new files.

What is in it: AR reaches any date and time with a Now button and daytime sun calibration,
bolder cardinal markers, a nearby cloud map behind "Somewhere nearby might be better",
pinch zoom on both maps, height at the pin, the real skyline on the Aurora tab, taller
meteor night columns, an honest star trails headline, a Credits and privacy page, and a
good deal of superfluous text removed.
